About Us

Aggie Propulsion & Rocketry Lab, APRL, is a student-run collegiate rocketry team at UC Davis, aiming to construct and launch a bipropellant liquid-fueled rocket, introducing liquid rocketry to the university for the first time in the institute’s history.

APRL aspires to make rocketry accessible to students of all backgrounds, regardless of any prior knowledge and experiences. Various subteams branching from vehicle design and propulsion systems teach and foster a community of like-minded individuals who all share a common passion for space and rocketry. We are committed to pioneering liquid rocketry for the day when cows fly.
