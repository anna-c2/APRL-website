Ophiotaurus

Brief description

Project Overview

Ophiotaurus (skim milk?, and from now on will be referred to as Ophio) is the final test rocket for before 2% Milk (aka taurus). It’s going to serve as a testbed for recovery and structural systems, along with building team skill in manufacturing the stringer-bulkhead airframe structures.

Ophio is planned to fly to an altitude of 2100 feet, and attain a maximum speed of close to 250 miles per hour. While that isn’t too high or fast, this will still provide us an invaluable opportunity to refine our manufacturing skills, hone our large rocket handling skills, and collect invaluable test data .
