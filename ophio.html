Ophiotaurus

Our test vehicle for DPF avionic and structural systems

Project Overview

Ophiotaurus is the final solid propulsion test rocket for before the complete liquid rocket. Serving as a testbed for recovery and structural systems, it will fly with much of the same hardware as the final liquid counterpart. The still provide us an opportunity to refine our manufacturing skills, launch procedures for this class of rocket, and collect dynamic data during flight and recovery.
